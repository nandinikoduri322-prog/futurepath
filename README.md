# 🎓 FuturePath

## Your Career. Your Future. Your Path.

**FuturePath** is an Education & Career Roadmap platform designed to help students understand their education and career options after Class 10.

## 🚀 What FuturePath Provides

- 🎓 Intermediate Streams
- 💻 Engineering Branches
- 📚 Diploma Courses
- 🔧 ITI & Vocational Courses
- 🧠 Skills & Projects
- 💼 Internship Guidance
- 🚀 Career Opportunities
- 🎓 M.Tech Pathways
- 🌎 MS Pathways
- 🤖 AI Assistant
- 🆘 Help Desk

## 🛣️ Career Journey

**Class 10 → Intermediate / Diploma / ITI → Higher Education → Skills → Projects → Internships → Career / Higher Studies**

## 💻 Technologies Used

- Python
- Streamlit

## 🌐 Live Website

👉 https://futurepath.streamlit.app

## 🎯 Purpose

FuturePath helps students explore different education pathways and make informed decisions about courses, skills, projects, internships and career opportunities.

## 👩‍🎓 Who Can Use FuturePath?

- School students
- Intermediate students
- Diploma students
- Engineering students
- Students looking for internships
- Students planning higher studies
- Students exploring career options
